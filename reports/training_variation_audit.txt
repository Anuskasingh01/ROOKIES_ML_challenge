F. Recommendations for Person 1 (Data Engineering & Normalization)
- Keep the current normalization. It boosts exact match rates safely.
- Do NOT try to normalize address strings heavily (like mapping St->Street) because variations are vast and complex; let fuzzy matching handle it.
- Missing addresses in S2/S3 are extremely common and must be handled downstream.

G. Recommendations for Person 2 (Blocking)
- Do NOT block purely on exact address match, as it fails frequently.
- Name match rate is much higher, but blocking purely on exact normalized name will miss pairs.
- Consider creating tokens/n-grams from normalized names for blocking.
- High-frequency names (like 'subway' or empty strings) should probably be blocked with geographic bins (state/country) to avoid candidate explosions.

H. What should NOT be changed in preprocessing
- Do NOT aggressively stem words. Do NOT attempt to correct spelling or expand abbreviations. It is too risky across multiple countries.
